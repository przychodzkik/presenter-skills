# Frameworks and sources

| Framework | What it says | Source | Tag |
|---|---|---|---|
| Information gap | Curiosity rises when people notice a gap between what they know and what they want to know | Loewenstein, "The Psychology of Curiosity" (1994) | research-backed |
| Tension and suspense | Tension comes from expectation and uncertainty about what happens next | Lehne & Koelsch, "Toward a general psychological model of tension and suspense" (2015) | research-backed |
| Curse of knowledge | Experts cannot easily imagine not knowing what they know | Newton's tapper-listener study (1990), reported in Heath & Heath | research-backed |
| System 1 / System 2, substitution, anchoring | Fast intuition answers an easier question than the one asked; first numbers shape later estimates | Kahneman, *Thinking, Fast and Slow* | research-backed (some priming results have not replicated; do not cite them) |
| Loss aversion | Losses weigh more than equal gains | Kahneman & Tversky, prospect theory | research-backed (the exact ratio varies) |
| Gestalt principles | Proximity, similarity and closure shape how people group what they see | Wertheimer and the Gestalt psychologists | research-backed |
| Picture superiority | Pictures are remembered better than words | Paivio, dual-coding theory | research-backed |
| Simple, Unexpected, Concrete, Credible, Emotional, Stories | Six traits of ideas that stick | Heath & Heath, *Made to Stick* | craft heuristic |
| Sinatra test | One example that makes a claim credible by implication | Heath & Heath, *Made to Stick* | craft heuristic |
| Story elements | Hero, desire, conflict, change | McKee, *Story* | craft heuristic |
| Sparkline | Alternate between "what is" and "what could be" to move an audience | Duarte, *Resonate* | craft heuristic |
| Slidedoc | A slide document made to be read, distinct from slides shown live | Duarte, *Slidedocs* | craft heuristic |
| SCQA | Situation, complication, question, answer | Minto, *The Pyramid Principle* | craft heuristic |
| Ladder of abstraction | Move between abstract claims and concrete examples | Hayakawa, *Language in Thought and Action* | craft heuristic |
| Declutter, focus attention | Remove non-data ink; use contrast to direct the eye | Knaflic, *Storytelling with Data* | craft heuristic |
| Slide roles (teaser, amplifier, punchline) | A slide has one job tied to a moment in the script | This set's own labeling | craft heuristic |
