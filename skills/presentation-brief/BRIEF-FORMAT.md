# presentation/brief.md

Create the `presentation/` folder in the working directory if it does not exist. Terms follow `presenting-vocabulary`. Every section is filled; write `open` where the user could not answer yet, with what is needed to close it.

```md
# Brief: <working title>

## Occasion
- Topic:
- Date and place (or platform):
- Talk length and Q&A length:
- Live or online, and whether the audience can interrupt:
- Room or audience size:
- Atmosphere (casual to formal), and who speaks before and after:

## Audience
- Who they are and what they do:
- What they already know about the topic:
- What the audience knows about the speaker:
- What they believe, fear or want without having said so:

## Before-state
What they struggle with or wrongly believe when they arrive.

## After-state
What they will know, have or intend to do when they leave. This is the payoff.

## Goal
Teach or persuade, and the speaker's own goals in up to three lines.

## Material
Everything the user brought: documents, data, stories, numbers, links, with one line each on what it is.

## Opening and closing ideas
- How the user imagines the start:
- How the user imagines the end:
- What the user most wants the audience to take away:

## Core message
One sentence, confirmed by the user.

## Style notes
Free text in the user's own words: length, simple or sophisticated language, tone, any speaker whose manner they like and what they like about it, things to avoid.
```
